One line, at most 200 characters: the phone battery's last text message to the charger it waited for all day. A judge model compares the lines two at a time and the funniest one takes the reward.

## The line

> u left me at 1% all day while u scrolled tiktok for 6 hours. plug me in within 10 seconds or i die mid-text and take your unsent 'i miss u' to the grave with me

160 characters. Left at 1% all day, ignored for six hours of scrolling — and now the battery is holding the unsent "i miss u" hostage.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (validated to 1–200 characters, non-blank, single line).
